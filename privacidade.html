Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This policy explains what data we collect when you purchase Abundance Frequency, what it's used for, and your rights, in accordance with Brazil's LGPD (Lei nº 13.709/2018) and, where applicable, other consumer privacy laws.

1. What we collect

  • Purchase information: your name and email address, collected at checkout to deliver your download and provide support.
  • Payment data: processed directly by Ticto — we do not store your card details ourselves.
  • Basic site analytics: aggregate visit data on our sales page, used only to understand what content resonates. We do not use third-party ad-tracking pixels within the product delivery itself.

3. How long we keep your data

We retain your purchase and email records for as long as needed to provide support and comply with tax/accounting obligations. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time, subject to legal retention requirements.

4. Your rights (LGPD)

  • Confirmation of what data we hold about you, and access to it;
  • Correction of incomplete or outdated data;
  • Deletion of your personal data;
  • Data portability;
  • Withdrawal of consent, where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, email contato@abundancefrequency.com (placeholder — replace with real support email before launch).

5. Security

We do not store payment card details. Access to your purchase records is limited to what's needed for order support.

6. Children

Abundance Frequency is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest version.
