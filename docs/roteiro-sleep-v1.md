# Roteiro-piloto v1 — "Sleep Reset for Abundance" (track 3 of 3)

> **Duração alvo narrado:** ~8-9 minutos, ritmo bem lento, indução ao sono
> **Uso sugerido:** ouvir deitado, luz apagada, antes de dormir

---

## [00:00] INDUCTION (~2min)

It's time to let the day go.

Get comfortable. Let your body sink into the bed. There's nothing left to do tonight — nothing to solve, nothing to plan.

Take a slow breath in... and a long breath out.

Again. Breathe in slowly... and let it go, longer this time.

With each breath, you're sinking a little deeper into rest. Your shoulders are heavy. Your hands are heavy. Your whole body has permission to stop holding on for tonight.

Whatever happened with money today — a worry, a number you didn't like, a decision you're unsure about — it can wait until morning. It's not yours to carry right now.

---

## [02:00] BLOCK — Releasing the day (~2min30s)

Picture today's money worries as small stones you've been holding in your hands all day.

One by one, imagine opening your hands and letting them fall away. You don't need to solve them right now. You don't need to hold them while you sleep.

Breathe in... and let one go.
Breathe out... and let another go.

Your hands are empty now. Open. Resting.

Tonight, your only job is to rest.

---

## [04:30] BLOCK — Soft affirmation (repeated slowly, ~2min30s)

As you drift, let these thoughts rest quietly in the background, like a soft hum:

*I am safe.*

*Tomorrow holds new possibilities.*

*I release what I cannot control tonight.*

*I trust that things are working out, even when I can't see how yet.*

*My mind is calm. My body is resting. Abundance flows to me while I sleep, just as easily as while I'm awake.*

There's nothing more to do. Just breathe, and let go.

---

## [07:00] CLOSING (drifting, ~90s, voice trails softer and slower)

You don't need to follow every word anymore.

Just breathe... slowly... and let your mind drift wherever it wants to go.

Rest now.
