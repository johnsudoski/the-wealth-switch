## Page 1 — Welcome

**Welcome to your 30-Day Abundance Ritual.**

This isn't about hoping for a miracle. It's about spending 7-9 minutes a day quieting the mental noise around money — so you can think, decide, and act more clearly.

Three tracks. One simple daily rhythm. Thirty days to build a habit that outlasts the program itself.

*A quick honest note: this guide and these audios are a relaxation and mindset practice. They are not financial, medical, or psychological advice, and they do not guarantee any financial outcome. What they're built to do is help you feel calmer and less reactive around money — consistently, if you use them consistently.*

## Page 2 — How To Use This Program

**The daily rhythm (pick what fits your day):**

| Time | Track | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Morning Money Mindset | 5 min |
| Anytime (main practice) | Abundance Frequency (Day 1-30, rotating themes) | 7-9 min |
| Night | Sleep Reset for Abundance | 8 min |

You don't need all three every day. If you only have 7 minutes, the daily main track is enough. Morning and Sleep are optional layers for when you want more.

**Where to listen:** somewhere you won't be interrupted. Headphones recommended, but not required.

**What NOT to expect:** don't listen once and wait for something to change. This works like brushing your teeth — the value is in the repetition, not any single session.

## Page 3 — Your 30-Day Map

**Week 1 (Days 1-7): Awareness & Release**
Noticing old scarcity patterns without judgment, and practicing letting them go.

**Week 2 (Days 8-14): Rebuilding Belief**
Working on the quiet, underlying belief that you deserve ease with money.

**Week 3 (Days 15-21): Action & Alignment**
Shifting from passive hoping to calm, clear decision-making.

**Week 4 (Days 22-28): Integration & Gratitude**
Making the calmer mindset feel normal — not a special practice, just how you think now.

**Days 29-30: Reflection**
Looking back at what shifted, and building your own after-the-program ritual.
