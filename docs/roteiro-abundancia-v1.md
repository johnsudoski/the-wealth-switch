# Roteiro-piloto v1 — "Abundance Frequency" (nome provisório)

> **Sub-nicho:** abundância financeira/prosperidade
> **Duração alvo narrado:** ~7-8 minutos (ritmo lento, meditativo, ~110-130 palavras/min)
> **Estrutura:** indução → repetição/afirmação (3 blocos) → fechamento — mesma lógica de "sessão diária curta" do Genius Song/Genius Switch
> **Idioma:** inglês (mercado ClickBank/Digistore24)
> **Guardrail aplicado:** nenhuma promessa de resultado financeiro garantido; framing é prática de mindset/relaxamento, não conselho médico ou financeiro — consistente com o risco já mapeado em 01-ESCOPO.md ("evitar claims de saúde/cura... nunca prometer resultado médico")

---

## [00:00] INDUCTION (relaxamento inicial, ~90s)

Find a quiet place, and let your body settle.

Close your eyes, or let your gaze soften downward.

Take a slow breath in... hold it for a moment... and let it go.

One more time. Breathe in slowly... and release.

With every breath, you're allowing your mind to slow down — away from the noise of the day, away from every worry about money, about bills, about what's not enough yet.

For the next few minutes, none of that matters. This is your time to reset.

Let your shoulders drop. Let your jaw unclench. Let your hands rest, open and relaxed.

You don't need to do anything right now except listen, and breathe.

---

## [01:30] BLOCK 1 — Releasing scarcity (~2min)

There's a difference between what you have and what you believe is possible for you.

Right now, gently notice any tension you carry around money — any old belief that says *there's never enough*, or *money is hard to come by*, or *people like me don't get to be wealthy*.

You don't have to fight that belief. Just notice it, the way you'd notice a cloud passing in the sky. It's there. And it can move on.

With this breath, imagine releasing that old story. Breathe in... and as you breathe out, imagine that tension leaving your body, like smoke dissolving into open air.

You were not born believing money was scarce. That belief was learned. And anything learned... can be unlearned.

Breathe in space. Breathe out the old story.

---

## [03:30] BLOCK 2 — Affirmation repetition (~2min30s)

Now, silently or softly with me, let these words settle into your mind. You don't need to force belief — just let the words be present, like seeds placed in soil.

*I am open to receiving abundance.*

*Opportunities find their way to me.*

*I make clear, confident decisions with money.*

*I am worthy of financial ease.*

*Abundance flows to me through expected and unexpected paths.*

*I release the need to struggle for what I deserve.*

*My mind is calm, and my future is expanding.*

Let those words rest in the background of your mind, like a quiet hum. You don't need to hold onto them tightly. Just let them be there.

---

## [06:00] BLOCK 3 — Visualization (~1min30s)

Picture yourself a few months from now. Not perfect, not unrealistic — just a little lighter. A little more at ease when you check your bank account. A little more confident when an opportunity crosses your path.

See yourself making one small, clear decision today that moves you one step closer to that version of you.

Hold that image gently. Let it feel possible, not distant.

---

## [07:30] CLOSING (~30s)

Take one more slow breath in... and let it go.

When you're ready, gently bring your awareness back to the room around you. Wiggle your fingers, your toes. Open your eyes when it feels right.

Carry this sense of ease with you into the rest of your day.

*This audio is a relaxation and mindset practice. It is not financial, medical, or psychological advice, and results are not guaranteed — everyone's experience is different.*
