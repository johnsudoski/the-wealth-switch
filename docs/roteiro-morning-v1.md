# Roteiro-piloto v1 — "Morning Money Mindset" (track 2 of 3)

> **Duração alvo narrado:** ~5 minutos, ritmo mais desperto que o track 1 (energizante, não sonolento)
> **Uso sugerido:** ouvir logo ao acordar, antes de checar celular/notícias

---

## [00:00] OPENING (~40s)

Good morning.

Before the day pulls you into emails, notifications, and everyone else's priorities — take thirty seconds for yourself.

Sit up. Take one full breath in through your nose... and let it out slowly.

This is the moment where you decide the tone of your day, before the world decides it for you.

---

## [00:40] BLOCK 1 — Setting the tone (~90s)

Today, you don't have to carry yesterday's money stress into this new day.

Whatever happened with your finances yesterday — a bill, a disappointment, a moment of doubt — that was yesterday. Today is a clean page.

Breathe in, and let your body feel a little lighter. Breathe out, and let go of anything from yesterday that doesn't need to come with you.

You're allowed to start today calm. You're allowed to start today believing something better is possible.

---

## [02:10] BLOCK 2 — Morning affirmations (~2min)

Say these with me, quietly, or just let them play in your mind.

*Today, I move through my day with a calm, clear mind.*

*I notice opportunities instead of obstacles.*

*I make decisions from confidence, not fear.*

*Money flows toward people who feel calm around it — and today, that's me.*

*I am building a life of ease, one clear decision at a time.*

*I trust myself to handle whatever today brings.*

Let those words settle. You don't need to force them to feel true yet — just let them be the first thing your mind hears today, instead of stress.

---

## [04:10] CLOSING (~50s)

One more breath in... and out.

Whatever your first task is today, walk into it a little lighter than you would have without these five minutes.

You're not behind. You're exactly where you need to be to take the next small step.

Have a good day.
